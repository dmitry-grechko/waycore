# Camera App

Camera capture and photo management.

## Features

- Photo capture with flash effect
- Front/back camera switching
- Gallery thumbnail preview
- Camera status monitoring

## Permissions

- `camera.capture` - Capture photos
- `storage.write` - Save photos

## Usage

The Camera app provides a simple interface for taking photos:
- Tap the shutter button to capture
- Switch between front and back cameras
- Tap the thumbnail to open Gallery
